In one sentence

Clearroom is an honest iPhone storage cleaner: it finds duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, old screenshots, big videos and duplicate contacts, entirely on the iPhone, with no weekly subscription.

Facts

PlatformiPhone, iOS 17 and later
PriceFree to scan and delete up to 30 items a day. Clearroom Pro: $39.99/year (14-day free trial), $6.99/month or $79.99 once.
PrivacyNo account, no servers, no ads, no analytics. Photos and contacts never leave the phone.
Made byOne independent developer

Why it exists

Popular cleaner apps often open with a frightening "scan", then ask for about $8 a week. Clearroom was built to be the opposite: real file sizes, a clear reason for every suggestion, nothing deleted without the user's confirmation, and a fair yearly price.

What it does

  • Duplicates and similar shots, with the best photo suggested and the reason shown
  • Blurry photos, receipts, screenshots and large videos
  • Swipe mode: keep or delete one photo at a time, month by month, with undo
  • Duplicate contacts merged into one card, with a backup and undo
