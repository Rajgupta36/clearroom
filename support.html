Deleting and getting photos back

I deleted something by mistake.

Open Photos, then Albums, then Recently Deleted. Items stay there for 30 days, and you can recover them from there.

Why didn't my free space go up right away?

iPhone frees the space when Recently Deleted is emptied, after 30 days or when you empty it yourself in Photos, Albums, Recently Deleted.

Will Clearroom ever delete something on its own?

No. Clearroom only suggests. Every delete goes through the iPhone's own confirmation dialog.

Photo access

Clearroom only sees some of my photos.

Go to iPhone Settings, Apps, Clearroom, Photos and choose Full Access. With Limited access, Clearroom can only check the photos you picked.

Does Clearroom upload my photos to check them?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. Clearroom has no servers. See the privacy policy.

Contacts

I want my old contact cards back.

Right after a merge, tap Undo merge. Later, open Clearroom's Settings, then Contact backups, and share the backup to Contacts.

Subscriptions and purchases

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Clearroom. Cancel at least a day before it renews.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Clearroom's Settings and tap Restore purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
