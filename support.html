Common questions

I deleted something by mistake.
Open Photos, then Albums, then Recently Deleted. Items stay there for 30 days.

Why didn't my free space go up right away?
iPhone frees the space when Recently Deleted is emptied, after 30 days or when you empty it yourself.

Clearroom only sees some of my photos.
Go to Settings, Apps, Clearroom, Photos and choose Full Access.

I want my old contact cards back.
Right after a merge, tap Undo merge. Later, open Clearroom's Settings, then Contact backups, and share the backup to Contacts.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Clearroom. Cancel at least a day before it renews.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?
Open Clearroom's Settings and tap Restore purchases.
