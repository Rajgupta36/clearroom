How it works

  1. Scan. Clearroom measures your library on the iPhone and shows how much space each category takes.
  2. Review. Open a category. Every group says why it was grouped, and the best photo is kept for you.
  3. Delete. Confirm in the iPhone's own dialog. Everything stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Questions

How do I delete duplicate photos on iPhone?

Open Clearroom and let it scan your library. Tap Duplicates to see every set of exact copies. Clearroom keeps one photo from each set and selects the rest; untick anything you want to keep, then tap Delete. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Is Clearroom safe? Do my photos leave my iPhone?

Your photos never leave your iPhone. Clearroom has no servers, no account, no ads and no analytics. All scanning happens on the device, and nothing is deleted until you confirm in the iPhone's own delete dialog.

How much does Clearroom cost?

Scanning is free, and you can delete up to 30 items a day for free. Clearroom Pro removes the limit for $39.99 a year (14-day free trial), $6.99 a month, or $79.99 once. There is no weekly plan.

How does Clearroom pick the best photo among similar shots?

It groups photos taken seconds apart that look almost the same, then suggests the sharpest, best-lit one using Apple's on-device image analysis. The reason is shown for every group, and you can tap any photo to keep it too.

Why didn't my iPhone storage go up after deleting photos?

iPhone keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. The space is freed when that album is emptied, either after 30 days or when you empty it yourself in Photos, Albums, Recently Deleted.

Can Clearroom merge duplicate contacts?

Yes. Clearroom finds contact cards that share a phone number, an email or a full name, keeps the fullest card, copies over anything the others have, and saves a backup first so you can undo the merge.

Are the storage numbers in Clearroom real?

Yes. Every size comes from the real file sizes of your photos and videos. If a size can't be read, Clearroom says so instead of guessing.
