Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version: Clearroom does not collect, store or share any of your data. Your photos, videos and contacts never leave your iPhone.

No data collectedNothing is sent to us or anyone else.
On your iPhoneEvery scan runs on the device.
No ads or trackingNo analytics or ad code at all.

Photos and videos

Clearroom asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos. All of this is done on your iPhone. Clearroom never uploads a photo, a video or any information about them.

If a photo is stored in your iCloud Photos and not on your iPhone, Clearroom may ask Photos for a small preview so you can see it. That preview comes from your own iCloud account to your iPhone. Nothing is sent from your iPhone.

Clearroom only deletes items you choose, using Apple's own delete confirmation. Deleted items stay in your Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

Contacts

If you use Duplicate contacts, Clearroom asks for access to your contacts so it can find cards for the same person. This happens on your iPhone. Before merging, Clearroom saves a backup of the affected cards on your iPhone so you can undo. Clearroom never uploads your contacts.

What Clearroom keeps on your iPhone

To open quickly next time, Clearroom saves file sizes, small fingerprints of photos and your last scan results in its own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

Clearroom has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code. It does not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Clearroom never sees your payment details. If you choose a trial reminder, Clearroom schedules a notification on your iPhone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

Clearroom doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
